# ADR 0003: Free MVP with no money competition

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-24

## Context

Entry fees, prizes, staking, betting, wallets, and payouts introduce legal, financial, fraud, safeguarding, reconciliation, tax, and security obligations. They would distract from proving tournament integrity.

## Decision

Version 1 is free. It includes no entry fee, wager, stake, wallet, cash custody, or platform-distributed prize. The team may later evaluate voluntary donations and non-competitive premium organizer tools after product trust and legal review.

## Consequences

The initial architecture is not a financial ledger. Future money features require a separate PRD, threat model, legal assessment, data model, controls, and ADR rather than adding amount fields to tournament records.
