# ArenaSports code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and community members must communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and behavior. We welcome people across nationality, ethnicity, gender, age, disability, religion, experience, and competitive level.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, hate, sexualized abuse, or discrimination;
- doxxing or exposing private match evidence;
- cheating assistance or result manipulation;
- impersonation and retaliation;
- spam or deliberate disruption;
- using contributor access to favor a player, organizer, or tournament;
- publishing a security vulnerability before responsible coordination.

## Enforcement

Report repository conduct concerns privately to the owner through the verified GitHub contact method. The owner may edit/remove content, restrict participation, or escalate credible safety threats. Reports should be handled with minimum necessary disclosure and conflicts of interest avoided.

Competition-user behavior is governed by future product terms and safety policy; this file governs project collaboration.
